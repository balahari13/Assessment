Questions

Straight answers, no brochure language.

Where does the work actually happen?

India, from home offices. We do not run a call-centre floor and we do not mix in unvetted freelancers. Agents pass a skills assessment and a WFH kit check (Windows PC, wired network, quiet space) before they sit on a live queue. Details: How a 100% WFH BPO in India works.

How fast can you go live?

A typical new process is designed to go live in about four weeks after you approve scope and grant system access. The clock pauses if logins, SOPs, or sample work do not arrive. See what those four weeks include.

Do you sign an SLA?

Yes. We will not start live work on “we’ll do our best.” The SLA names response or handling targets, quality sampling, reporting day, and who we call when we are going to miss. If you have no baseline, we set a pilot target and tighten it after real volume.

How do you protect client data?

Least privilege, named accounts (no shared “training” IDs in production), patched Windows endpoints, wired networks, and a written incident path. Access is removed the day someone leaves. Longer note: data protection on a remote floor. Legal summary: privacy notice.

What languages and hours do you cover?

English is the default for client-facing work. Other languages are scoped per engagement — we do not list languages we cannot staff. Hours follow your peaks, including extended coverage from India, not a single 9-to-5 IST block unless that is what you need.

What does a proposal include?

We acknowledge an enquiry within one business day and aim to send a written proposal within 48 hours. It covers scope, roster, tools, security assumptions, timeline, and commercial outline. Email info@trinitasnxt.in or use the form.

Can we start with a small pilot?

That is the default. We prefer one queue or one process with extra sampling before we add headcount. Scaling is a decision after the pilot meets the bar — not a contractual surprise in week two.

Who is my day-to-day contact?

One named delivery owner. Weekly ops notes go to your named owner. If an SLA is at risk, that person contacts you before the weekly pack. Escalation to leadership is in the SOW so you are not hunting for a founder on a weekend.

How do you hire agents?

Candidates register on Careers, complete a skills assessment, and — if invited — book a weekday interview. Healthcare WFH has extra kit rules on the healthcare page. We do not publish scores to candidates; recruitment reviews them internally.

Are you a large, old BPO?

No. Trinitas NextGen Business Solutions Pvt. Ltd. was founded in 2026. We are honest about that. You get a remote-first India team and leaders who still sit close to the work — not a legacy floor with a new logo. Read the CEO note.

Do you work only with enterprises?

We work with mid-market and enterprise teams that can name an owner, share real sample work, and live with an SLA. If the need is a handful of hours with no process, we will say we are not the right fit.
