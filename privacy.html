Your Data, Protected

We treat your information as a trust — not a commodity.

This short notice explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we care for it when you use our website, careers tools, assessments, or contact forms.

Who we are

Trinitas NextGen Business Solutions Private Limited (“Trinitas”, “we”) operates this website and related hiring and client inquiry tools. Contact: info@trinitasnxt.in.

What we collect

  • Inquiries — name, work email, company, message when you request a proposal
  • Careers — registration details, assessment responses, optional resume files and cover notes
  • Employee portal — name, generated employee email, applications, tasks, and internal messages
  • Technical basics — limited technical data needed to run the site securely (for example session or browser storage for assessments)

Why we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and prepare proposals
  • To run recruitment and skills assessments fairly
  • To support onboarding and internal employee tools
  • To improve reliability and security of our services

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use career or assessment information for unrelated marketing lists.

How we protect it

We protect information the way a long-term partner should:

  • Need-to-know access — only people who need data for hiring or delivery can reach it
  • Careful handling — practical controls for remote work, secure endpoints, and documented workflows
  • Purpose limits — resumes and assessments are used for recruitment and operations, not casual sharing
  • Admin safeguards — sensitive dashboards require sign-in

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose you shared it for — for example, to complete recruitment review, respond to an inquiry, or maintain an active employee account — or longer where law or a legitimate operational need requires it. Paused assessment sessions and OTPs are time-limited.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete personal data we hold about you, subject to legal or operational requirements (for example, completed assessment records already under review). Email info@trinitasnxt.in with your request and we will respond carefully.

Third parties

We use trusted infrastructure to host the site and process forms (for example hosting and form delivery services). Those providers process data only to deliver the service we configured — not to buy or sell your identity.

Updates

We may update this notice as our tools grow. The current version will always be available on this page. Last updated: July 2026.
